## About

I am an undergraduate in Integrated Circuit Design and Integrated Systems
at Soochow University. My interests lie in digital hardware, FPGA
architecture, electronic design automation, and the design and
optimization of computing systems. I am particularly curious about the
connections between low-level circuits and the architectures they make
possible. As I continue learning, I hope to better understand how
circuits, design tools, and architectural choices come together to
shape a computing system.

## Research Interests

### Core Interests

- **FPGA Architecture**  
  Programmable logic and the architectural foundations of
  reconfigurable computing.

- **Electronic Design Automation**  
  Methods and tools for designing, analyzing, and optimizing
  digital circuits.

- **Digital Integrated Circuit Design**  
  Digital logic, RTL design, and the relationship between
  hardware descriptions and circuit implementation.

- **Computing Architectures**  
  The organization of computing systems, with an interest
  in AI hardware and acceleration.

### Interdisciplinary Interests

- **Hardware–Software Co-Design**  
  Exploring how hardware and software choices inform one another.

- **Coherent Optical Communication**  
  An interest in the connections between optical communication,
  digital hardware, and signal processing.

- **Neuromorphic & Connectome-Inspired Hardware**  
  Exploring how neural organization may inform computing architectures.

- **AI-Assisted Chip Design**  
  Understanding the potential role of AI in hardware design
  and design automation.

## Current Exploration
